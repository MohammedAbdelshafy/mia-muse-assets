## Mia teaches

Teaching videos presented by Mia as a social teaching persona ("Professor Mia" — a social label only, not a product). These are AI-presented teaching explainers, not recordings of live product actions.

Format: episodes 1–4 are lip-synced to their voiceovers (local lip-sync pass). From episode 5 on, the series is **write format only** — the lesson is carried by board writing plus bold captions; Mia does not speak on camera, with no lip-sync and no talking head (founder rule, 2026-10-28). Episode 5 was remade in write format; the write-format file below is the current one.
